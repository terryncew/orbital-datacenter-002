# Verification — per-claim results

Independent verification of the science in this repository. All values
below are from executed code (`python3 simulation/run_reproduction.py`),
not from any earlier version's text. Where an earlier version disagreed
with the executed code, the code wins and the version's text is listed as
a correction.

## Reproduction

- The C6 falsification audit, the 16-arm tournament, the 216-corner sweep,
  the crossover sweep, and the TVAC coupon CAD verification all reproduce
  deterministically: two consecutive regenerations produce byte-identical
  evidence JSON.
- The 8-test suite passes in standard and `-O` modes with zero warnings.
- The recovered screen reproduces C0 = 21.85, C1 = 17.95, C6 = 11.93 kg/kW
  at 1 MW, and the included recovered files verify against the included
  manifest (hashes computed at runtime).
- The public evidence JSON in `evidence/` is generated by running the shipped
  code (`python3 simulation/run_reproduction.py`); it is not copied from any
  earlier file. Two consecutive runs produce byte-identical evidence, and
  `run_reproduction.py` prints its deterministic SHA-256 on every run.

## Corrections to the earlier version (D-002-1..D-002-12)

1. **D-002-1: Held-out replay numbers stale.** The earlier version's
   numbers (Arm D $32,126.74, Arm O $16,668.42, −48.12%) do not
   match the executed code (Arm D $40,405.04, Arm O $22,038.35, −45.46%
   at 8 MW, 550 km, 53° LEO, $350/kg). The replay is excluded from the
   paper entirely: it was produced by an internal engine not in this
   package and carries no tournament science. No replay numbers appear
   in PAPER.md.
2. **D-002-2: Internal evaluation ledger stale.** Ledger entry 04 said Arm O
   $339.6M/MW at $0/kg vs Arm A $262.0M/MW, 62% reduction, $0.118/kWh;
   executed: $257.28M vs $220.0M, 45%/0.30× conditions, $0.135/kWh
   (itself unreproduced — see D-002-13). Ledger entry 01 said repaired C6
   21.65 kg/kW (+81.5%); executed 19.44 (+62.95%) screen / 20.66 Arm G.
   Ledger entry 03 said Arm N 15.10 kg/kW and 39.4% cut; executed 14.58 and
   36.7%. The paper uses executed values throughout.
3. **D-002-3: "22.29 kg/kW modeled in Arm B" withdrawn.** Appears only as
   a hardcoded string in an earlier falsification script; no code computes
   it. The paper states the all-in continuous range as assigned,
   19.05–24.80 kg/kW, and the test suite asserts the withdrawn figure
   appears nowhere in the audit output.
4. **D-002-4: Arm A below BCG's terrestrial range.** Arm A $220M/MW sits
   below BCG's $230–300M/MW (BCG Aug 2026, verified live). The paper
   discloses this and shows ratio sensitivity: at BCG low end $230M Arm O
   is 1.185×; midpoint $262M → 1.04×; high end $300M → 0.91× (orbital
   wins). BCG orbital is $660–750M/MW at today's costs, not 002's modeled
   future costs — not presented as validating 002's absolutes.
5. **D-002-5: SpaceX AI1 envelope instability.** On 24 Aug 2026
   new.spacex.com served a smaller envelope the same day: 150 kW peak /
   120 kW avg / 70 kW/ton / 20 m × 70 m (17.86 kg/kW continuous). The 001
   paper used the 150 kW envelope from the June 2026 SEC filing. This
   paper uses the www envelope (250 kW) and states the alternate.
6. **D-002-6: F002 adders are assumed inputs, not measurements.** The
   Redwire iROSA flysheet does not publish a per-m² boom mass. The
   "5 assumptions" wording is corrected to 4 screen steps plus end-of-life
   degradation in Arm G.
7. **D-002-7: Regenerated figures not byte-identical to earlier PNGs.**
   Figure 1 panel A y-labels were truncated (raw code names);
   figure 1 panel B x-labels garbled ("Round 1 Arm N (Arm G)",
   "Round 2 Arm O (800V Spine[20yr swap)"); figure 2 panel B showed only
   9 of 16 arms with no stated selection. Public figures are regenerated
   with fixed labels by `simulation/generate_figures.py`.
8. **D-002-8: Black-carbon factor differs 001 (0.042) vs 002 (0.020
   kg/kg).** Ledger totals are not comparable across studies; the paper
   states this.
9. **D-002-9: Cost scale-invariance is an assumption.** No learning curve
   or volume discount; $272.65M/MW at 1/4/16 MW is assumed, not found.
10. **D-002-10: Arm P net depends entirely on the assumed $4,850/kW-yr
    credit.** Reported strictly separately from unsubsidized C3.
11. **D-002-11: Al₂O₃ fractions assigned** (0.48 default, 0.09 Arm O/P),
    not measured.
12. **D-002-12: 001 vs 002 method reconciliation.** Full table in PAPER.md
    Section 4.3: 001 is a 5-yr, $1,500/kg, 1/150/1,000 kW study with no
    silicon refresh (terrestrial $1,384.33/kW-yr, best orbital
    $10,495.60/kW-yr, ratio 9.72× on TFLOPS-yr/$); 002 is a 20-yr,
    $500/kg, 1/4/16 MW study with $45,000/kW-IT per 5-yr generation
    (terrestrial $12,077.26/kW-yr, Arm O $14,960.66/kW-yr, ratio 1.239×
    on $/kW-yr). Absolute $/kW-yr figures are NOT comparable across
    studies (8.7× apart, driven by the silicon-refresh accounting). Only
    within-study ratios are comparable, and even those use different
    metrics. The ratio shrank mainly because the common $180M/MW silicon
    cost compresses ratios toward 1.0. 001's 60% thermal claim and 3.06×
    scenes/$ claim are 001-only; 002 does not re-derive them.

## Additional findings (found by executing the code)

13. **D-002-13: The $0.135/kWh electricity parity threshold is not
    reproduced — WITHDRAWN and replaced by a computed breakeven.** The
    crossover routine stated terrestrial firm clean electricity ≥ $0.135/kWh
    as a parity condition. Direct evaluation: Arm A at $0.135/kWh costs
    $12,811.88/kW-yr, still below Arm O at $500/kg ($14,960.66) and below
    Arm O at $0/kg ($14,117.33). The public code now computes the breakeven
    by bisection instead of asserting it: $0.3310/kWh at $500/kg launch,
    $0.2541/kWh at $0/kg launch (test_06 asserts both and asserts the 0.135
    field is absent from the crossover output). The paper states the computed
    breakevens and names $0.135/kWh as withdrawn.
14. **D-002-14: The Arm H "3.92× Arrhenius acceleration" figure is not
    reproduced.** The code's Arrhenius multiplier at 126 °C vs the 88 °C
    reference (E_a = 0.70 eV) is 8.51×, giving an effective annual failure
    rate capped at 0.35/yr (vs 0.046/yr for Arm E). The paper uses the
    executed rates and does not repeat 3.92×.
15. **D-002-15: Arm O non-silicon cost at $0/kg.** The packet states
    $4,235.93/kW-yr (1.929× terrestrial non-silicon $2,195.86). Executed:
    $4,240.42/kW-yr (1.931×). The paper uses the executed value.

## Model assumptions disclosed in the paper

- Silicon $45,000/kW-IT per 5-yr generation, identical on Earth and in
  orbit; the common $180M/MW compresses cost ratios toward 1.0.
- Nominal compute density 22 TFLOPS/kW (dense FP16). Absolute
  TFLOPS-yr/$ scales with it; ratios do not.
- Cost scale-invariance is an assumption (no learning curve, no volume
  discount).
- F002 repair adders are assumed engineering inputs, not measurements.
- Arm P's $4,850/kW-yr credit is a hardcoded assumed input, not a market
  price.
- Al₂O₃ ablation fractions (0.48 default, 0.09 Arms O/P) are assigned.
- Black carbon 0.020 kg per kg launched; CO₂e = 14.5 t per tonne launched
  + 680 t fixed (rough model). Stratospheric ERF/ozone multiplier UNKNOWN.
- Starship 665/905 m³ volumes are computed from the 2020 Users Guide
  dimensions, not guide-quoted figures; the guide is preliminary and the
  bound is not a certified usable payload.
